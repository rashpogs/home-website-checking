The Heart of LIKAS

LIKAS means something natural, inherent, or something that is already part of us. In our game, the name represents the natural Filipino trait of being likas na matulungin, makatao, at mapagmalasakit.

During disasters such as typhoons and floods, Filipinos show their true spirit of bayanihan by helping one another, rescuing those in danger, sharing resources, and providing hope to those who have lost their homes.

The word LIKAS can also be associated with likas or paglikas, which means evacuating or moving to a safer place during emergencies. This connects directly to our game's main theme, where players are challenged to make decisions, respond to emergency situations, and understand the importance of proper evacuation, preparedness, and helping others.

Through LIKAS, we want to show that surviving a disaster is not only about saving ourselves but also about extending a helping hand to those who need it. It reminds us that although disasters may test our strength, our compassion and willingness to help are what make us truly Filipino.

"LIKAS: Likas na Katatagan, Bayanihan, at Malasakit.
Dahil ang pagtulong sa kapwa ay hindi lamang tungkulin, kundi likas na katangian ng isang tunay na Pilipino."
